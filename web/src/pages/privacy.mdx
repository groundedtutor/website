## 2. What personal information is collected?

The personal information collected depends on how you use the website and services. It may include:

- Contact information, such as your name, phone number, email address, and service address for in-home visits.
- Booking details, such as appointment date, service type, preferred contact method, and notes about the issue you want help with.
- Payment and billing information, such as invoices, payment status, and transaction references. Full payment card details should be handled by a payment processor and not stored by {import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME}.
- Service history, such as services provided, general troubleshooting notes, follow-up reminders, and customer preferences.
- Device and technical information, such as device type, operating system, software, internet connection details, printer model, or error messages.
- Information visible during a support session, such as emails, contacts, photos, documents, account settings, browser tabs, or files, but only to the extent needed to provide the requested service.
- Website usage information, such as pages visited, form submissions, browser type, approximate location, and analytics or cookie information if enabled.

{import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME} does not intentionally collect more personal information than is reasonably necessary to provide services, manage bookings, process payments, communicate with customers, and meet legal or business record requirements.

## 3. Sensitive information and account access

Technology support can sometimes involve sensitive information. {import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME} follows these boundaries:

- You should enter your own passwords, PINs, and one-time codes whenever possible.
- {import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME} does not store customer passwords, password manager master passwords, banking PINs, two-factor authentication codes, recovery codes, or full payment card numbers.
- You should be present during account access whenever possible.
- {import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME} will not ask for unnecessary access to your email, banking, photos, documents, or private files.
- {import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME} does not provide financial, legal, medical, tax, or investment advice.
- {import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME} will not help a relative, caregiver, or other person access another adult’s device or account unless that adult has clearly authorized the service or valid legal authority is provided.

If you are uncomfortable with anything visible on your screen or device during a session, you may pause or stop the service at any time.

Personal information may be used to:

- Respond to questions and service requests.
- Schedule, confirm, reschedule, or cancel appointments.
- Provide in-home, phone, or remote technology support.
- Prepare for a support session and understand the issue you need help with.
- Process payments, issue receipts, and maintain business records.
- Provide follow-up notes, reminders, or ongoing support plan services.
- Improve services, website usability, and customer experience.
- Protect customers and the business from fraud, misuse, security issues, or legal claims.
- Meet legal, tax, accounting, regulatory, insurance, or record-keeping obligations.

Personal information will not be sold. It will not be used for unrelated marketing without appropriate consent.

By contacting {import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME}, submitting a booking form, using the website, or receiving services, you consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information as described in this policy and as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service.

If remote support is used, you may be asked to install or open a remote support tool. During a remote session, {import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME} may see your screen and may control your device with your permission.

- You should close private files, photos, browser tabs, or messages that are not related to the service.
- You may end the remote session at any time.
- {import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME} will explain material changes before making them where practical.
- Remote sessions are not recorded unless you are told in advance and separately agree.
- Any temporary remote access code or session permission should expire or be removed after the session where the tool allows.

- Remote support, troubleshooting, security, backup, or cloud service providers used to deliver the service.
- Device manufacturers, internet providers, software providers, or other support providers when you ask for help contacting them or when needed to troubleshoot an issue.
- Insurers, legal advisors, accountants, regulators, law enforcement, or government authorities where required or permitted by law.

Some service providers may process or store information outside Quebec or outside Canada. Where required, {import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME} will consider whether appropriate privacy protections are in place before using those services.

{import.meta.env.PUBLIC_SITE_NAME} uses reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information. These may include:

- Keeping business devices reasonably protected with updates, access controls, and security software.
- Avoiding storage of customer passwords, banking credentials, recovery codes, or unnecessary sensitive notes.
