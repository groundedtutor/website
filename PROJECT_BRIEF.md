# Project Brief: Friendly Tech Support Website (Montreal)

## 1. Project Overview

Build a friendly, accessible marketing website for a Montreal-area tech support service focused on older adults and people who feel less confident with everyday technology.

The website should communicate patient, plain-English help in a warm, reassuring, trustworthy, and judgment-free tone. Visitors should feel comfortable reaching out for support with computers, phones, tablets, email, video calls, online safety, backups, printers, Wi-Fi, smart home devices, and general tech questions.

## 2. Objectives

- Explain services clearly in plain English.
- Increase booking inquiries for remote, phone, and in-home support.
- Build trust with clear privacy, safety, and boundary messaging.
- Reduce decision anxiety with a simple service-selection helper.
- Make the website highly usable for older adults (readability, clarity, accessible interactions).

## 3. Target Audience

Primary audiences:

- Older adults in Montreal and nearby areas.
- Family members booking support for parents or relatives.
- Anyone who feels overwhelmed by technology.
- People who prefer patient one-on-one guidance over jargon-heavy support.

## 4. Core Positioning

Technology should make life easier, not more stressful.

This service offers friendly, one-on-one tech help:

- In-home (Montreal and nearby areas)
- By phone
- Online/remote

Delivery method should be presented as based on the issue type and customer comfort level.

## 5. Service Area Messaging

- In-home visits: Montreal and nearby areas.
- Travel fees may apply outside the main service area.
- Remote and phone support may be available anywhere in Canada when suitable.

## 6. Content and Information Architecture

Implement as either:

- A single-page website (preferred), or
- A small multi-page structure

Recommended one-page section order:

1. Hero
2. Why choose this service
3. Services
4. Ongoing plans
5. How booking works
6. Service area
7. Trust, privacy, and safety
8. Not sure what to book?
9. Contact / booking CTA

## 7. Hero Section Requirements

Headline:

- Friendly Tech Help for Older Adults in Montreal

Subheadline:

- Patient, plain-English support at home, by phone, or online.

Primary CTAs:

- Book a Tech Help Session
- Ask What Service Is Right for Me

## 8. Main Services (Required)

### 8.1 Friendly Tech Help Session

Price: Starting at $79 remote or $119 in-home.

Use for specific tech problems, everyday questions, troubleshooting, or confidence building.

Include example use cases:

- Slow computer, phone, or tablet
- Email problems
- Printer issues
- Wi-Fi or Bluetooth problems
- Help using an app or website
- Video calls with family, doctors, or appointments
- General technology questions

Good for:

- First-time support
- Everyday tech questions
- Troubleshooting
- Building confidence

### 8.2 New Device Setup

Price: Starting at $99 remote or $149 in-home.

Use for new phones, tablets, computers, printers, smart TVs, streaming devices, and smart home devices.

Include example scope:

- Basic device setup and updates
- Email, contacts, apps, and account sign-in
- Transferring contacts, photos, or files where possible
- Printer, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and accessory setup
- Accessibility settings (larger text, voice controls, screen readers, simplified layouts)
- Smart home setup (doorbells, cameras, lights, thermostats, viewing apps)

Required note:

- Customers must provide account passwords, unlock codes, and pay any third-party subscription costs.
- Extra devices or complex transfers may require more time.

### 8.3 Online Safety and Scam Protection Review

Price: Starting at $119 remote or $159 in-home.

Use for concerns about scams, suspicious emails, fake websites, hacked accounts, passwords, online banking safety, or social media privacy.

Include example scope:

- Password and account safety review
- Help setting up a password manager
- Two-factor authentication setup
- Recognizing scam emails, calls, texts, and fake websites
- Social media privacy settings
- Basic device security and anti-malware review
- Safer online shopping and banking habits

Required disclaimer:

- This service helps reduce risk, but no technology service can guarantee complete protection from scams, fraud, malware, or account compromise.

### 8.4 Photo, File and Backup Organization

Price: Starting at $119 remote or $159 in-home.

Use for protecting memories, organizing documents, and making files easier to find.

Include example scope:

- Reviewing current backups
- Setting up cloud or external-drive backups
- Organizing photos, videos, and documents
- Email cleanup and file-saving guidance
- Scanning old photos where equipment and time allow
- Creating simple folders or albums that are easy to understand

Required disclaimer:

- Backup services reduce the chance of data loss but cannot guarantee every file can be recovered, transferred, or preserved.

## 9. Ongoing Support Plans (Required)

### 9.1 Technology Confidence Plan

Price: $39/month or $99/quarter.

Include:

- One scheduled remote or phone support session each month (up to 30 minutes)
- Priority booking when available
- 10% off in-home visits and larger service packages
- Friendly reminders for updates, backups, password reviews, and online safety habits

Positioning guidance:

- Best for people who want ongoing reassurance and a familiar person to contact before small issues become frustrating.

### 9.2 Backup and Safety Check Plan

Price: $19/month plus any third-party software or storage costs.

Include:

- Periodic help checking backups
- Password manager health checks
- Device update checks
- Basic security settings review

Required disclaimer:

- This is not continuous security monitoring and does not replace professional cybersecurity services.

## 10. How Booking Works (Required)

Include a simple step-by-step section:

1. Customer explains what they need help with.
2. Service recommends the best option and whether remote or in-person is better.
3. Customer chooses an appointment time.
4. Technician explains changes before making them.
5. Customer receives simple next steps after the session.

## 11. Remote vs In-Home Guidance (Required)

Remote support is best for:

- Software issues
- Email
- Video calls
- Settings
- Guidance and learning

In-home support is best for:

- Printers
- Wi-Fi
- Smart devices
- Accessibility setup
- Hands-on learning

Also include:

- Some issues may require parts, third-party accounts, manufacturer support, or specialist repair shops.

## 12. Trust, Privacy, and Safety Messaging (Required)

Create a dedicated trust section with reassuring, clear language.

Must include these points:

- Customer privacy matters.
- Service may help access accounts, adjust settings, or organize files only as needed.
- Customers should be present during account access whenever possible.
- Service will not ask for banking PINs, card numbers, or unnecessary passwords.
- Customers remain responsible for approving changes to accounts, files, devices, and subscriptions.
- No financial, legal, medical, or investment advice is provided.
- No guarantee that scams, fraud, malware, or data loss can be fully prevented.
- Backups, transfers, and account recovery depend on device condition, account access, storage availability, and third-party services.
- Personal information for booking and service records must be handled according to applicable Canadian and Quebec privacy requirements.

## 13. Decision Helper: Not Sure What to Book?

Include a simple mapping block:

- My computer is slow. -> Friendly Tech Help Session
- I bought a new phone, tablet, or computer. -> New Device Setup
- I am worried about scams or passwords. -> Online Safety and Scam Protection Review
- My photos and files are everywhere. -> Photo, File and Backup Organization
- I want ongoing help. -> Ongoing Support Plan

## 14. Tone, Voice, and Writing Style

Use:

- Warm, calm, plain-English language
- Short paragraphs and clear headings
- Friendly reassurance
- Non-judgmental wording

Avoid:

- Unexplained jargon
- Fear-based language about scams
- Overly technical or corporate tone

## 15. UX and Accessibility Requirements

Design for older adults and accessibility-first use:

- Large readable text and strong hierarchy
- High contrast body text
- Large, clearly labeled buttons
- Simple navigation and predictable layout
- Service cards with clear pricing and outcomes
- Generous spacing and uncluttered pages
- Clear focus states and keyboard accessibility
- Semantic headings and landmarks
- Alt text for icons/images where meaningful

## 16. Visual Design Direction

The visual feel should be:

- Trustworthy
- Local
- Friendly
- Calm
- Accessible
- Professional but not corporate

Suggested style implementation:

- Soft colors with high contrast text
- Simple service icons
- Plenty of white space
- Easy-to-read cards and pricing labels
- Prominent phone/contact options

## 17. Required Calls to Action

CTAs to include across sections:

- Book a session
- Call for help
- Ask a question
- Get help choosing the right service
- Schedule in-home or remote support

## 18. Compliance and Disclaimer Requirements

Include clear, friendly disclaimers that state:

- Services do not provide financial, legal, medical, or investment advice.
- Scam protection and security reviews reduce risk but cannot guarantee complete protection.
- Backup and file transfer services cannot guarantee every file can be recovered or preserved.
- Customers must provide access to accounts, passwords, unlock codes, and required third-party subscriptions where needed.
- Personal information should be handled according to applicable Canadian and Quebec privacy requirements.

## 19. Suggested Implementation Deliverables

Minimum deliverables:

- Final page structure (single page or small multi-page)
- Complete approved copy for all required sections
- Reusable service and plan card components
- Booking process section with 5-step flow
- Trust/privacy/disclaimer section
- Decision helper block
- Footer with contact options and disclaimer access

## 20. Acceptance Criteria

A build is considered complete when:

- All required sections in this brief are present.
- All required services, prices, and disclaimers are included.
- Tone is plain-English, warm, and non-technical.
- Site supports easy decision-making for non-technical users.
- CTAs are prominent and repeated in key sections.
- Accessibility basics are implemented (readability, keyboard support, semantic structure, contrast-minded styling).
- Service area and remote/in-home guidance are clearly explained.
- Privacy and limitation statements are visible and easy to understand.
